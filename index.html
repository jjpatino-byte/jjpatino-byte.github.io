About Me

I am a robotics researcher interested in enabling robots to perceive, reconstruct and interact with unknown environments. My work combines robotic motion planning, active perception, optimisation and 3D reconstruction. I am currently pursuing a PhD at Cardiff University.

My broader interests include robot manipulation, impedance control, redundant manipulators and intelligent robotic systems.

Research Interests

View Planning

Selecting informative and reachable camera viewpoints to improve the reconstruction of unknown objects.

Motion Planning

Planning feasible robotic trajectories while accounting for kinematic constraints, obstacles and reconstruction objectives.

Robotic Control

Control of robotic manipulators, including impedance control and redundant robot systems.

3D Reconstruction

Active perception and optimisation methods for reconstructing unknown objects from RGB-D data.
