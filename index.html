About me

I am a civil engineer with a Master of Science in Civil Engineering from Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University (Virginia Tech) and a bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral (ESPOL), Ecuador.

My academic and research experience includes Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, environmental impact assessment, and geospatial analysis, particularly in assessing mining-related environmental and social impacts in the Ecuadorian Amazon.

My research interests extend to hydrology, watershed management, water resources, natural hazard mitigation, and engineering solutions that support public health and vulnerable communities.

Master’s project · Virginia Tech · May 2025

SMART SCALE: Scoring System Replication and Transportation Funding Analysis

Built a Python model to replicate Virginia’s SMART SCALE project evaluation system, analysing historical funding patterns and simulating 100 transportation projects. The model achieved R² = 0.9916 against official scores and highlighted a potential scoring preference for highway projects over pedestrian, cycling, and multimodal alternatives.
